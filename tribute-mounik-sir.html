Our Story

How I Met Him

🤝

I met him during an internship, through online classes. I noticed that his examples were excellent and his teaching skills were great — he spoke to all of us students in a friendly way, so we always had a fun time.

I am proud to have had a teacher like him, who felt like a friend.

Written For Him

Poem, Specially for Sir

Goodbye, Sir 😭😭

You taught us more than words can say, You guided us along the way. With every lesson, every smile, You made our journey worthwhile. You were a teacher, you were a guide, A helping hand always by our side. Your words will stay within our heart, Even when we are far apart. The classroom may feel quiet and new, But we will always remember you. The lessons you gave, the care you shared, Will remind us how much you cared. So, goodbye, Sir, as we depart, Your memories will remain in our heart. Wherever life may lead us to, We will always be thankful to you. Goodbye, Sir. Thank you for everything 🌸🌸😊😊.

But sadly 😥 our internship comes to an end — Sunday, 30th August was our last class with Sir, a fun ending to our internship classes. 😭😭
